1968-1972 Corvette Wiper Arm Adjustment

Wiper Arm Adjustment 68-72 Corvette

1968-1972 Corvette Windshield Wiper Setting (Adjustment) Procedure.

1) Install rod and blade assembly to transmission. Note: Key on transmission locates rod and blade assembly in positive location.

2) Install linkage to motor crank with ball in center of slot.  Finger tighten the nuts-part of transmission assembly making sure there is grease between the crank ball and the brass plates.

3) Position the tip of LH blade 1” below the park stop bracket.

4) Position the tip of RH blade above nd touching LH blade.

5) Torque nuts listed in step 2.

6) Position blades on upper surface of the park stop bracket

7) Cycle the system on a wet windshield and adjust the tips of the blades to .75” from the edge of the molding using Screw A as shown in the diagram below.
