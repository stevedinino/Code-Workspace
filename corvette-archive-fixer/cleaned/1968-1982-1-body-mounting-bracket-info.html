1968-1982 #1 Body Mounting Bracket Info

1968-1982 #1 Body Mounting Bracket Info: The body mount bracket used on C3 cars in the number one position is very difficult to replace. There is no access to the rear reinforcement (5) in the picture below.

If you don’t want to hack away at your firewall or remove the outer fender to gain access it is suggested that you grind off the outer rivet heads leaving the center one in place to support the inner bracket. You’ll need to make a judgement call on this by looking at the condition of the old rivet heads. If the head is excessively corroded in the center then it’s advised to choose a different rivet to leave. Next run a drill through the four holes you removed the heads from so you can remove the material left in the hole by the old rivet.

Once you have done this you should be able to remove the head from the rivet you left and then install the new bracket using only the four rivet holes you have just drilled. Once you have the four rivets in place then run your drill through the hole from the last one you ground off using the new bracket on the outside as a guide.

Pop rivets work best for this but if you are doing a NCRS car you’ll pretty much need to gain access to the back side for riveting or fabricate some sort of rivet that had the same outside appearance. This is not a repair for the meek that’s for sure.
