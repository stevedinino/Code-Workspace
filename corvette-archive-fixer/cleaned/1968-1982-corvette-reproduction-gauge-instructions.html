1968-1982 Corvette Reproduction Fuel Gauge, Temperature Gauge, Oil Pressure and Battery Gauge Instructions * Read before you install.

If installing reproduction gauges, please read this first.  Failure to read the instructions that come with the gauge can result in your gauge not working properly.

If you purchased reproduction gauges DO NOT use the original resistors with the gauge installation.  The resistance is already built into the gauge and using another resistor will cause the gauge to fail.

This picture is the reproduction instructions sheet included with the gauges. I have a very hard time reading this so I felt making a larger size sheet would be beneficial to everyone. Please test your original gauges before you purchase new, there is a ton of info on this site for how to do this including videos.

Electrical parts are not returnable even if they do not appear installed.
