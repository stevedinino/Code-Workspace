1968-1982 Corvette Windshield Frame Installation Updated 2017

Supporting Documentation <-click here to read the original article

At the time I wrote this there was only a few small issues with the upper header, then they got worse.  How, I have no idea but the headers got so bad that we could only use them as a repair header and not a replacement header.

Then two years ago we got with the manufacturer for over a year trying to find the flaws in the radius of the header.  After probably 15 installations of the test product the flaws were finally corrected.   The current production of the header not only has the correct radius, it also has the mirror mounting holes in the correct place.
