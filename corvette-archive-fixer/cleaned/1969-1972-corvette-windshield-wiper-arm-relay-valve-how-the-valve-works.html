1969-1972 Corvette Windshield Wiper Arm Relay Valve. How the Valve Works

Below is a picture that shows you how the 1969-1972 Corvette Windshield Wiper Arm Relay Valve works. Always remember you do not test from the lower port, you test from the middle port. If the valve is in the open position as shown on the left side, vacuum should be blocked on the lower port, if the valve is in the collapsed (arms down), the valve should allow vacuum to pass only from the middle port to the lower port.

Please be aware:

If you purchased the plastic Migty Vacuum pump, the odds are that it will leak vacuum. We’ve purchased two of these in the last year and while both would pump vacuum, both would not hold vacuum.

If you search Youtube you’ll find there is another video done by another vendor that shows the valve being tested on the lower nipple and not the middle on. You can not test the valve in this manner. Just because the valve blocks vacuum on the lower nipple doesn’t mean it won’t leak out of the top of the valve. Testing for blockage on the middle nipple is the only true way to test this product.
