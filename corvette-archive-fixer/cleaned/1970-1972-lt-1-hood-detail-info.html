1970-1972 LT-1 Hood Detail Info

Early 1970s up to s/n 9590 have a silver background outlined in black. All others have a white background outlined in black.

1970-1972 LT-1 Hood Decal detail.  The pictured decal is a NOS Gm decal with Silver and black colors.  Note: the flaw in the top portion of the leg on the L.  This flaw is in both sides.
