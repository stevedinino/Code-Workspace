Exhaust Manifold Notes

Cast-iron exhaust manifold casting dates may not be visible when manifold is installed due to date location. Some casting numbers may not be visible when installed due to equipment blocking view. Casting date codes are generally the same as described for engine blocks, except some may not normally carry a calendar-year reference. Those manifolds used with AIR (Air Injection Reactor) emissions equipment are drilled and tapped for AIR tubing. Manifolds generally contain a cast-in “LH” (left-hand) or “RH” (right-hand) designating specific installation location.
