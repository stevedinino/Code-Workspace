1976 Interior Color Change

The pictures below illustrate the quality of a new interior installed in this 1976 Corvette, the color is 1972 red.  The seats were custom made in that color as well as the dash pads, The stock 1972 door panels were used and all said, it turned out quite nice! The car has a DIN style radio bezel with a new Kenwood radio.  Everything in the interior was either restored or replaced.
