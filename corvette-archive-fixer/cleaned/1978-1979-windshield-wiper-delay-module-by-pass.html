1978-1979 Windshield Wiper Delay Module By-pass.

To by-pass the 1978-1979 wiper delay control module.

You must first access the module which is located under the center dash console. It’s easiest to access this by removing the driver side shift console panel.

Remove the connectors from the module, the module connectors will look like the ones shown above only they are made to the controller harnesses.  Once unplugged, jumper the wires the connectors above plug into, shown above only the connectors made on the harness.
