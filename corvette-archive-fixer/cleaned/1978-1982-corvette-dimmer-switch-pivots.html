1978-1982 Corvette Dimmer Switch Pivots ** Some in Stock **

*** ****  They are discontinued and there is currently no reproduction! Limited supply

Dimmer Switch Pivots 78-82. There were three design’s of this pivot and they will not interchange. This is what breaks when the turn signal lever is broken or damaged. We have two original pivots in stock at the time of posting, the links are listed below.  The dimmers were as follows:  1978 early, 1978 Late – 1979 early and 1979 late – 1982.   You can see from the picture below that the GM parts catalog did not list an early or late 1979 pivot.   So we have conflicting information, but to my knowledge the early 1979 cars used the late 1978 pivot.  The angle of the plastic was different.  But it is also my belief that the 1979 cars could all use the 704 pivot with the difference being the angle of the arm wouldn’t be correct.
