1978-1982 Speedo and Tach Housing Tilt Needed to Install With Column

To install or remove the tach and speedo housing, you must remove the lower steering column cover, remove the two column bolts and push very hard downward on the column.

Then put the housing in place by tilting it as shown in the picture below.  If you take your time you can do this.

To reduce the possibility of scratching the steering column, you may want to place a towel on top or even some painters tape.
