Corvette Rear Carpet Installation 1968-1977

This picture was lightened to show how well the ACC carpet fit in the rear of the car.

In the picture below you can see an original 1968 carpet rear riser, these were both the same for 1968 and 1968 and measured 22″ as shown in the picture.  This carpet did not tuck under the rear compartment forward edge, the jute shown in the picture forced the lower edge of the “flip over” section of carpet to press against the forward edge of the rear storage compartment.
