Corvette Spoiler Extension Picture 1979 Only W/L-82, AC HD Cooling, And No Urethane Spoilers

Spoiler Extension Picture 1979 Only W/L-82, AC HD Cooling, and no Urethane spoilers. As you can see in this picture the rubber extension actually riveted to the ABS lower spoiler supplied with all cars not having the urethane spoiler option. While the picture shows a different style rivet, the original rivets were of the “POP” design with a head about 1/2″ in dia. There were also two metal strips behind the ABS spoiler that helped pinch the ABS spoiler between the retainers and the rubber extension.
